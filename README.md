# PM in the Gen AI Era — Jason Xu

A 15-minute presentation exploring how generative AI reshapes product management, team boundaries, and the organizational delivery factory.

Built with the **Founder's Journal** slide system (Washi cream, warm ink-black, and vermillion hanko seal red). No build step, no dependencies — pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JS.

---

## 15-Minute Timing & Slide Guide

| Slide | Section | Duration | Key Message & Speaking Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Title** | 0:30 | Setup and intro: *PM in the Gen AI Era* — Jason Xu. |
| **2** | **About Me** | 0:45 | I am a product manager turned builder, with 10+ years in scaled-out distributed systems, bringing modern UX to traditional enterprise software, and now helping teams become AI-native. Key phrases ink in one by one. |
| **3** | **Big Tech vs. Solo Builder** | 1:30 | Contrast headlines: Big Tech (Google / Alibaba / Oracle) vs. Solo Builder (PrepPal / Lunatechs / Fluffy). |
| **4** | **Table of Contents** | 0:45 | Agenda: Part 01 — The PRD is Dead. Part 02 — AI-Native Teams Will Destroy Traditional Teams. |
| **5** | **Three Jobs Called PM** | 0:45 | Program manager (spreads best practices across the org) vs. project manager (gets one project done on time) vs. product manager (ships features that solve a user's problem). This talk is about the product manager. |
| **6** | **The Bridge** | 1:15 | The Bridge graphic: User & Painpoint &darr; [The PRD] &darr; Engineering Org. Press next once to strike through the PRD, then again to advance. |
| **7** | **The Question** | 1:30 | Setup: Sales codes demo, Designers ship A/B tests, Engineers complete MVP. Punchline: *What does the PM do?* Press next to bring in Sales, Designers and Engineers one at a time, then the question. |
| **8** | **PMs = Process + Problem** | 1:15 | The Old World (*PMs had to do both*) vs. The Gen AI World (*PMs own the problem*). As process machinery drops to zero, the PM's entire focus returns to customer truth. |
| **9** | **Where Do You Spend Your Time?** | 1:15 | 2x2 Matrix of the individual PM, process vs. problem focus. (Top-Left: The Danger Zone / Busy, but replaceable; Top-Right: The Old World / You do both, the slow way; Bottom-Left: Low Impact / Not moving the product; Bottom-Right: High Impact / You own the problem). |
| **10** | **The AI-Native PM** | 1:15 | The way out of the danger zone. The 3 pillars: 1. Empathy (painpoints), 2. Judgment (taste & skills), 3. Medium (communicate in "product"). |
| **11** | **Pillar 2 Divider** | 0:45 | *AI-Native Teams Will Destroy Traditional Teams.* Transition to the organizational collapse. |
| **12** | **The Two Forces** | 1:15 | Two forces destroying traditional companies: 1. Code is nearly free, 2. Agents work 24/7. Each force appears on its own press. |
| **13** | **The Token Bottleneck** | 1:15 | Meetings are now the token bottleneck: meetings run at 3–5 tokens/sec, a single agent at 100 tokens/sec. Press next to bring in the agent; its number counts up to 100. |
| **14** | **Client Delivery Org** | 1:30 | The Client Delivery Organization. Before: Product → Design → Engineering, meetings are the handoff. Press next for Now: one team per client, demo by the end of the meeting. |
| **15** | **Pick Your Class** | 1:30 | RPG archetypes for your role in the Client Delivery org: The Beastmaster (Agent Swarms), The Knight (Frontline FDE), The Mage (Business Architect). Each class appears on its own press. |
| **16** | **Two Ways Forward** | 1:00 | Two ways forward for the PM: The Apple Way (show them what they're missing) vs. The Google Way (quickly iterate on the idea). *Both start with the user.* |
| **17** | **Recap** | 1:00 | The short version: Part 01 (PMs own the problem, communicate in product) and Part 02 (code is nearly free, silos collapse into Client Delivery, both ways forward start with the user). |
| **18** | **Questions & Discussion** | ~2:00+ | Q&A, LinkedIn QR code, and final thoughts. |
